# ? buySmarty Chrome Extension

Real-time Price History Tracker, Fake Discount Detector & Cross-Store Deal Finder for Google Chrome (Manifest V3).

---

## ? Key Features

1. **Auto-Detecting Store Scanner**:
   - Seamlessly recognizes when you are viewing any product on **Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Meesho, or Ajio**.
   - 1-Click analysis directly from the Chrome extension popup.

2. **In-Page Floating Widget (Content Script)**:
   - Floats unobtrusively on product pages (`Amazon`, `Flipkart`, `Myntra`, `Meesho`, `Ajio`).
   - Click **`? buySmarty Deals`** to slide open real-time 30-day price trends, seller authenticity, and cross-store pricing.

3. **Right-Click Context Menu**:
   - Highlight any product title or right-click any link to instantly trigger **"Track on buySmarty"**.

4. **Universal Search**:
   - Paste any product URL or search keyword across all 5 supported stores.

---

## ?? How to Install in Google Chrome (Step-by-Step)

1. Open **Google Chrome** on your computer.
2. In the address bar, type:
   ```text
   chrome://extensions
   ```
   and press **Enter**.
3. In the top-right corner, turn **ON** the toggle for **`Developer mode`**.
4. In the top-left corner, click the **`Load unpacked`** button.
5. In the file picker dialog, navigate to your project folder and select:
   ```text
   D:\College\Buy-Chhatke\Extension
   ```
6. **That's it!** ?? The `buySmarty ? AI Price Intelligence` icon will appear in your Chrome toolbar.
7. Click the **Pin ??** icon next to buySmarty in your extensions menu to keep it visible.

## ?? Testing the Extension

1. Ensure your local backend server is running (`http://localhost:3000`).
2. Visit any product on [Amazon.in](https://www.amazon.in), [Flipkart.com](https://www.flipkart.com), [Myntra.com](https://www.myntra.com), [Meesho.com](https://www.meesho.com), or [Ajio.com](https://www.ajio.com).
3. Notice the floating **`? buySmarty Deals`** badge at the bottom-right of the webpage.
4. Click the badge or open the extension popup from your toolbar to analyze prices in real-time!
